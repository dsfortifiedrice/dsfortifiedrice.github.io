DS Fortified Rice Industries LLP

India’s Grain, Prepared for the World

DS Fortified Rice Industries LLP is a vertically integrated rice company based in Malerkotla, Punjab. We handle sorting, processing, packing and export of Basmati and Non-Basmati rice, and we also supply 100% broken rice for industrial use and pest free / organic rice.

We work with importers, distributors and food companies who want consistent grain, clear communication and reliable shipments.
